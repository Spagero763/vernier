What it does
Multiplies the pool's own price by the factor the token has already published.
Why not quote a target
A fixed price gets drained the moment the token trades at a discount. Multiplying leaves the market's opinion on the curve.

What guards it

The rate
Can only move as fast as elapsed time allows. A fake spike is clamped, not believed.
The operator set
EigenLayer operators can flag a rate as untrustworthy. That is all. They cannot set a price.
